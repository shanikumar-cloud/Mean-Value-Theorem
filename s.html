Main Theorem Formula
f'(c) = f(b) − f(a) b − a
Conditions: If a function f(x) is continuous on the closed interval [a, b] and differentiable on the open interval (a, b), then there exists at least one point c ∈ (a, b) where the tangent line is parallel to the secant line connecting (a, f(a)) and (b, f(b)).
